Step-by-Step Voting Flow

01

Nikalne Se Pehle

Voter list mein apna naam, polling station ki location, aur zaroori ID documents pehle hi check kar lein.

02

Center Pe

Official line mein khade hon, verification ke liye ID taiyar rakhein, aur polling staff ke niyam follow karein.

03

Booth Ke Andar

Bina kisi distraction ke apna vote privately dalein, apni choice dhyan se check karein, aur aaram se bahar aayein.

04

Vote Ke Baad

Jo bhi mark ya slip confirmation mile use lein, aur apni pocket guide ya civic notes sambhal kar rakhein.

Taiyari Ki Checklist

Do's vs. Don'ts

Do's (Kya Karein)

  • Zaroori ID saath le jaayein aur bheed se bachne ke liye jaldi pahunchein.
  • Process se judi kisi bhi asli madad ke liye authorized polling officials se poochein.
  • Line discipline banayein rakhein aur voting area ko shant aur vyavasthit rakhein.

Don'ts (Kya Na Karein)

  • Mana ki gayi cheezein saath na le jaayein aur booth privacy ke niyam na todein.
  • Bahar ki afwahon ya unofficial baaton mein bilkul na aayein.
  • Vote dalte waqt jaldbazi na karein—options ko shanti se padh kar hi select karein.

Main Voter Rights & Samman

Secret Ballot Ka Adhikar

Aapka vote bilkul private hai. Booth ke andar koi aapko zabardasti nahi kar sakta aur na hi dekh sakta ki aapne kisko vote diya.

Fair Treatment Ka Adhikar

Har eligible voter ko polling center par izzat aur bina kisi bhedbhav ke barabar vyavhar milna chahiye.

Saaf Jankari Ka Adhikar

Aap official staff se basic voting rules aur process ke baare mein saaf aur sahi sawal pooch sakte hain.

Aam Myths vs. Facts

Myth

"Pehli baar vote dalna confusing hota hai aur sirf experts ke liye hai."

Fact

Yeh process kaafi simple steps mein hota hai—details check karna, ID verify karna, aur chupchap vote dalna.

Myth

"Agar mujhe kisi rule par shak hai, toh main basic madad nahi maang sakta."

Fact

Polling staff aur civic guides isliye hi hote hain taaki voters ko sahi rules aasani se samajh aayein.

Jhatpat Jawab (FAQs)

Ghar se nikalne se pehle mujhe kya-kya double-check karna chahiye?

Official voter list mein apni details check karein, zaroori ID taiyar rakhein, aur apne polling center ki location confirm kar lein.

Booth ke andar main apna vote private kaise rakh sakta hoon?

Designated voting space ke andar akele aur dhyan se apna vote dalein aur normal privacy rules follow karein.
